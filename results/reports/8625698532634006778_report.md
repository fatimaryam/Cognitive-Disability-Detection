## Clinical Findings

These findings underscore the need for considering all three tests' patterns concurrently to provide meaningful insights.

## Clinical Impression
The overall drawing pattern is not suggestive of Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI). The combined assessment demonstrated an estimated probability of 94.7% for Normal cognition and 5.3% for Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI). This interpretation is based on the integrated evaluation of all three drawing tasks rather than any individual drawing.


## Recommendation

Ongoing observation may assist in monitoring cognitive status over time, especially given the current findings which require close follow-up to detect any subtle changes.

These findings should be interpreted together with the patient's clinical history, neurological examination, and formal cognitive assessment, and should not be considered diagnostic based solely on the drawing tasks.
