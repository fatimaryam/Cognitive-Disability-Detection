## Clinical Impression
The overall drawing pattern is not suggestive of Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI). The combined assessment demonstrated an estimated probability of 95.3% for Normal cognition and 4.7% for Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI). This interpretation is based on the integrated evaluation of all three drawing tasks rather than any individual drawing.


## Recommendation
Ongoing observation may assist in monitoring cognitive status over time should any changes become evident.

These findings should be interpreted together with the patient's clinical history, neurological examination, and formal cognitive assessment, and should not be considered diagnostic based solely on the drawing tasks.
