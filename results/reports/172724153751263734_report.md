# Cognitive Drawing Assessment Report

**Patient ID:** 172724153751263734

## Cognitive Drawing Assessment
Evaluation of all three tasks revealed that the clock drawing accounted for the majority of the total contribution (90.8%), followed by the trail making test (4.1%) and the copy drawing (5.1%). The clock drawing also exhibited the largest proportion of localized atypical regions (25.0%), notably higher than both the copy drawing (3.5%) and the trail making test (8.4%).

## Clinical Findings
Quantitative analysis demonstrated modest quantitative variation across the three drawing tasks, reflecting differences in both contributions and localized atypical regions. While there was notable variability, particularly in the clock drawing, the integrated assessment indicates that individual performances cannot be isolated as definitive indicators of cognitive impairment.

These findings should be interpreted together with the patient's clinical history, neurological examination, and formal cognitive assessment, and should not be considered diagnostic based solely on the drawing tasks.
