The overall drawing pattern is not suggestive of Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI). This interpretation is based on the integrated evaluation of all three drawing tasks rather than any individual drawing.


## Recommendation

Ongoing observation may assist in monitoring cognitive status over time should any new cognitive concerns arise.
