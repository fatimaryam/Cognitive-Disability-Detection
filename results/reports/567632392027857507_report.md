## Clinical Findings

While each task showed some degree of atypicality, particularly prominent in the clock drawing, this variation underscores the need for comprehensive interpretation.

## Clinical Impression
The overall drawing pattern is not suggestive of Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI). The combined assessment demonstrated an estimated probability of 95.2% for Normal cognition and 4.8% for Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI). This interpretation is based on the integrated evaluation of all three drawing tasks rather than any individual drawing.


## Recommendation
Clinically reassessment could be undertaken if future cognitive concerns emerge, ensuring a thorough evaluation remains a priority.

These findings should be interpreted together with the patient's clinical history, neurological examination, and formal cognitive assessment, and should not be considered diagnostic based solely on the drawing tasks.
