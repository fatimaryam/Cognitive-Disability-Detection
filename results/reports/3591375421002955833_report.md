## Clinical Findings

Evaluating the three drawing tasks reveals quantitative variation but no clear patterns indicative of specific cognitive impairments. Each task contributes differently, reflecting modest differences in observed anomalies, particularly marked in the trail making drawing.

## Clinical Impression
The overall drawing pattern is not suggestive of Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI). The combined assessment demonstrated an estimated probability of 95.5% for Normal cognition and 4.5% for Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI). This interpretation is based on the integrated evaluation of all three drawing tasks rather than any individual drawing.


## Recommendation

Further assessment may be appropriate if cognitive symptoms progress. Routine clinical follow-up may be considered should cognitive concerns arise.

These findings should be interpreted together with the patient's clinical history, neurological examination, and formal cognitive assessment, and should not be considered diagnostic based solely on the drawing tasks.
