## Clinical Impression
The overall drawing pattern is not suggestive of Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI). The combined assessment demonstrated an estimated probability of 88.8% for Normal cognition and 11.2% for Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI). This interpretation is based on the integrated evaluation of all three drawing tasks rather than any individual drawing.


## Recommendation
Routine clinical follow-up may be considered should cognitive concerns arise.

These findings should be interpreted together with the patient's clinical history, neurological examination, and formal cognitive assessment, and should not be considered diagnostic based solely on the drawing tasks.
