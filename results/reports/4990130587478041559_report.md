## Clinical Findings

Quantitative analysis demonstrated moderate variability across the three drawing tasks, with no single finding standing out as clearly indicative of specific cognitive patterns. ## Clinical Impression
The overall drawing pattern is not suggestive of Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI). The combined assessment demonstrated an estimated probability of 91.7% for Normal cognition and 8.3% for Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI). This interpretation is based on the integrated evaluation of all three drawing tasks rather than any individual drawing.


## Recommendation

Routine clinical follow-up may be considered should cognitive concerns arise.

These findings should be interpreted together with the patient's clinical history, neurological examination, and formal cognitive assessment, and should not be considered diagnostic based solely on the drawing tasks.
