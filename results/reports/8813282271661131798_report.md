# Cognitive Drawing Assessment Report

**Patient ID:** 8813282271661131798

## Cognitive Drawing Assessment

The integrated drawing assessment revealed that the clock drawing made the greatest proportional contribution (97.3%) compared to the trail making and copy drawings which had minor contributions (1.5% and 1.2%, respectively). Additionally, the clock drawing displayed the highest proportion of localized atypical regions (23.5%), followed by the trail making drawing (14.5%) and then the copy drawing (2.3%).

## Clinical Findings

Quantitative analysis demonstrated modest variability in the quantitative observations across the three drawing tasks. While there was notable representation of atypical regions particularly prominent in the clock drawing, no single finding independently established clear cognitive impairment.

## Clinical Impression
The overall drawing pattern is not suggestive of Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI). The combined assessment demonstrated an estimated probability of 94.9% for Normal cognition and 5.1% for Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI). This interpretation is based on the integrated evaluation of all three drawing tasks rather than any individual drawing.


## Recommendation

Clinical reassessment can be undertaken if future concerns emerge regarding the patient’s cognitive function. Routine clinical follow-up may be considered should cognitive concerns arise.

These findings should be interpreted together with the patient's clinical history, neurological examination, and formal cognitive assessment, and should not be considered diagnostic based solely on the drawing tasks.
